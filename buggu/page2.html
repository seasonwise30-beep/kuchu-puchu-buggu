A Little Letter for You

Some feelings deserve their own little page. ♡

Dear Buggu, 💌

Pata hai, kabhi-kabhi kuch feelings ko words mein explain karna bahut difficult hota hai. Dil mein bahut kuch hota hai, par samajh nahi aata ki shuru kahan se karein.

I just want you to remember that your feelings matter. You don't always have to pretend to be okay, and you don't have to have every answer figured out.

I hope you always find space to be yourself — to laugh at silly things, talk about your worries, share your dreams, and enjoy even the smallest moments.

Aur haan, tumhari chhoti-chhoti efforts bhi important hain. Har baar perfect words ki zaroorat nahi hoti; kabhi-kabhi genuine care hi sab kuch keh deti hai. 💙

If a day feels heavy, take it one step at a time. If something bothers you, it's okay to talk about it. And if you need some quiet time, that's okay too.

I hope our little world becomes a collection of happy memories, honest conversations, silly jokes, and moments that make us smile whenever we remember them. 🌙

You don't have to be perfect to be appreciated. Just being yourself is enough to make you someone worth celebrating.

Take care of yourself, keep believing in your dreams, and never forget to appreciate the person you're becoming. 🧸✨

With lots of warmth and little blue hearts,
Your Buggu's favorite person 💙
