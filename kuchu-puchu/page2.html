Things I Can't
Always Say

Heyyy, meri Kuchu Puchu! 🥹💗

Pata hai, kabhi-kabhi hum apni feelings ko words mein express nahi kar paate. Dil mein bahut kuch hota hai, par jab kehne ki baari aati hai, toh samajh hi nahi aata ki shuru kahan se karein.

Isliye aaj socha, tumhe kuch aisi baatein bata doon jo shayad main normal days mein itni achhe se nahi keh paata.

Tumhari chhoti-chhoti baatein, tumhara random sa message aur tumhari silly si harkatein kabhi-kabhi ek ordinary moment ko bhi special bana deti hain.

Aur sabse achhi baat? Tumhe har baar kuch extraordinary karne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Tumhara bas tum hona hi kaafi hai. 💗

Main nahi chahta ki tum kabhi yeh socho ki tumhari baatein ya tumhari feelings matter nahi karti. They do. More than you might realise.

Ho sakta hai main har baar perfect words na dhoondh paun, par main chahta hoon ki tumhe pata rahe ki tumhari presence appreciated hai aur tumhari happiness important hai.
